# Annual Leave Policy

Full-time employees accrue 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2 days per completed month of service.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused leave days may be carried over into the next calendar year.
Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited.

## Approval
All leave requests require manager approval at least 7 days in advance.
